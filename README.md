A single-page portfolio deck (16 main slides plus an appendix) covering my data analysis case studies, built as one self-contained HTML file. My focus is risk, compliance, and fraud analytics: finding data that departs from its own pattern and turning it into decisions that can be acted on.
Case studies included
Risk and control
Procurement Risk Prioritization (1.3M Indonesian government contracts, LPSE 2013 to 2023, Python, PostgreSQL, Power BI) · repo
Ranked 518 public buyers by risk against their own market norm (observed vs expected), so no buyer is penalized simply for what it buys. 170 buyers stayed above norm in two separate periods.
Credit Risk and Control Effectiveness, Lending Club (2.26M loan records, Python, PostgreSQL, Tableau) · repo
Tested whether income verification actually lowered default risk, controlling for risk grade and cohort year.
